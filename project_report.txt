SOLAR ENERGY GEOGRAPHICAL CLUSTERING REPORT
============================================================

1. Objective
To cluster geographical areas based on solar energy production and support strategic business decision-making.

2. Methodology
- Data cleaned and aggregated at ZIP code level.
- Features included total and average energy production, system size, storage size, and project counts.
- Models compared: KMeans, Agglomerative Clustering, Gaussian Mixture.
- Best model selected: KMeans with 2 clusters.

4. Recommendations
- Prioritize high-production ZIP clusters for sales targeting.
- Use medium-production zones for expansion feasibility studies.
- Investigate low-production zones before allocating resources, as they may have lower historical output or fewer installations.
- Combine these cluster results with market, land, and policy data for stronger project planning.
